InstagramUnfollowers

See who doesn't follow you back.

Copy the code, paste it in the console on instagram.com, and you get the list. It runs in your own browser, and it never asks for your password.

    With the button above. It's the whole tool, in one line.

  2.

    Go to instagram.com on a computer and log in.

  3. 
        Paste it in the console
        
    Open it with Ctrl Shift J, or ⌘ ⌥ J on a Mac. Paste and press Enter.
